Endings & Saves

How it works

Three ending trophies are needed: Age of the Stars, Elden Lord and Lord of Frenzied Flame. You get one ending per playthrough, so either back up your save or play three times.

Route A · one playthrough with backups

  1. Follow the route to the end of phase 24 with Ranni's quest done and Mohg, the Omen dead. Don't inherit the Frenzied Flame.
  2. After Godfrey / Hoarah Loux, rest at the Elden Throne grace, quit to the title screen, and back up your save.
  3. Beat the final boss and use Ranni's blue summon sign → Age of the Stars.
  4. Restore the backup. Beat the final boss and mend the Elden Ring without Ranni's sign → Elden Lord.
  5. Restore again. Warp to Cathedral of the Forsaken, hit the wall behind the altar, drop to the bottom, remove all armour and inherit the Frenzied Flame from the Three Fingers. Beat the final boss → Lord of Frenzied Flame.
Backing up. Quit to the title screen first; the game autosaves constantly.
PC / Steam: close the game and copy %APPDATA%\EldenRing somewhere safe. Copy it back to restore.
PS4 / PS5: Settings → Saved Data → upload to cloud (PS Plus) or copy to USB.
Xbox: manual backups aren't possible, so use Route B.

Route B · NG, NG+, NG++

Start NG+ from the giant round table in the Roundtable Hold after the credits. Each extra run only needs the shortest path:

  1. Get two Great Runes (Godrick and Rennala are easiest).
  2. Reach Leyndell via the Grand Lift of Dectus (both medallion halves) or the Ruin-Strewn Precipice. Or: kill Radahn, go Nokron → Siofra Aqueduct → Deeproot Depths, beat Fia's Champions and use the teleporter.
  3. Beat Godfrey's shade and Morgott.
  4. Beat the Fire Giant and burn the Erdtree.
  5. Beat the Godskin Duo and Maliketh in Farum Azula.
  6. Beat Gideon, Hoarah Loux and Radagon / Elden Beast, picking the ending you still need.

Keep the Frenzied Flame for its own run. Once inherited it overrides every other ending.

Things that can lock you out

Fia: don't kill her before you've fought Lichdragon Fortissax.
Ranni: never give her Seluvis's Amber Draught. It ends her quest, the Dark Moon Greatsword and Age of the Stars.
Bolt of Gransax: pick it up in the Royal Capital before burning the Erdtree.
Miquella's Needle: after inheriting the Frenzied Flame, using it in Placidusax's arena permanently removes the Frenzied Flame ending.
